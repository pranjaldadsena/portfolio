AI · Civic Safety · Prototype

JanRakshak AI

A concept platform for detecting, analysing, and managing public-safety incidents — with separate citizen and administrator workflows, incident analysis, voice interaction, and severity classification.

AI-orientedIncident classificationVoice interactionCitizen + admin workflows
Read the case study
Problem
Public-safety incidents are reported inconsistently, and there's no single workflow connecting a citizen's report to an administrator's response.
Approach
Split the product into two workflows — a citizen-facing side for reporting and safety information, and an administrator side for triage — connected by incident analysis and severity classification.
Built
The core application structure covering both workflows, voice interaction for reporting, and a live safety-statistics view.
Status
Prototype / concept — not a deployed or government-adopted platform.
Learned
Designing for two very different users in one product forces much clearer thinking about what each screen is actually for.

MATLAB · DSP

GUI Guitar Tuner

A graphical tuning app that uses autocorrelation to find the fundamental frequency of an audio signal and compares it against target guitar notes.

AutocorrelationDSPGUI
Read the case study
Problem
How do you estimate the fundamental frequency of a guitar note from raw audio?
Approach
Autocorrelation to find periodicity in the signal, then map the detected frequency to the nearest target note.
Built
A MATLAB GUI that visualises the detected frequency against the target and shows how far off it is.
Learned
Signal processing stops being abstract the moment it becomes something you can tune a real string against.
